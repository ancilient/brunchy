Delete your Brunchy account

Brunchy student app, by Ancilient Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Brunchy and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with Delete. You are signed out straight away.

If you have an order that is still being prepared or waiting at the counter, collect or cancel it first.

Without the app

Email connect.ancilient@gmail.com from the Google account you use with Brunchy, with the subject Delete my Brunchy account. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • The link to your Google account and all your signed-in sessions.

What is kept

  • Bills and payment records of orders you already paid for, without your name or contact details, for as long as accounting, tax and legal rules require (see the Privacy Policy).
  • A log entry recording that the account was deleted.

If you sign in again later with the same Google account, Brunchy starts a new, empty account.
