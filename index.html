Brunchy

Skip the canteen queue. Order in 30 seconds.

What Brunchy does

Brunchy is a campus canteen pre-ordering app by Ancilient Technologies Pvt. Ltd. Students browse the menu of their canteen's stalls, pay once on their phone, and get a separate bill with a 3-digit pickup code for each stall. When the food is ready, they say the code at the counter and collect it.

Stall staff use the Brunchy Vendor app to receive paid orders, mark them ready and hand them over.

How to use it

  • Students: sign in with your Google account, choose your canteen, add items, pay, and wait for the "Ready" notification.
  • Stall staff: sign in to the Brunchy Vendor app with the Google account registered for your stall.

Sign-in

Brunchy uses Sign in with Google. We only receive your name, email address and Google account ID, which we use to create and secure your Brunchy account. See the Privacy Policy.
