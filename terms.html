The service

Brunchy lets students pre-order and pay for food from campus canteen stalls and collect it at the counter with a pickup code. The food itself is prepared and served by the stall.

Your account

  • You sign in with your Google account and are responsible for activity on your Brunchy account.
  • Stall staff must use only the account registered for their stall.
  • We may suspend accounts that misuse the service, for example repeated uncollected orders, fraud or abuse.

Orders and payment

  • Prices, taxes and any convenience fee are shown before you pay. Amounts are calculated by Brunchy.
  • Items are held for you for 5 minutes while you pay. If payment is not completed in that time, the items are released.
  • Each stall's part of your order is a separate bill with its own number and pickup code.
  • Keep your pickup code private: the stall hands the food to whoever gives the correct code.

Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel a bill before the stall starts preparing it; that bill is refunded to your original payment method.
  • If a stall rejects your order or an item is unavailable, the affected amount is refunded automatically.
  • Food that has been prepared but is not collected is not refunded.
  • Refunds are processed by our payment provider and may take a few working days to appear.
