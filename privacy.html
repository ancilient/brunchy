Privacy Policy

Brunchy and Brunchy Vendor apps

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Brunchy student app and the Brunchy Vendor app ("Brunchy", "we", "us") collect, how we use it and the choices you have. Brunchy is operated by Ancilient Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Information we collect

  • Google account information: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We do not receive your Google password, contacts, files or any other Google data.
  • Orders: the canteen and stalls you order from, items, quantities, prices, bill numbers, pickup codes, order status and the times your order was placed, prepared and collected.
  • Payments: payment status, amount and the payment reference from our payment provider. Card, UPI and bank details are entered with the payment provider and are not stored by Brunchy.
  • Stall staff: for vendor accounts, the stall you work at and actions you take in the Vendor app (for example accepting, marking ready or handing over an order), which we log for audit.
  • Technical data: basic information needed to run the service securely, such as sign-in session tokens, and, if notifications are enabled, a device notification token.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To take orders, reserve stock, process payments and refunds, and show your bills and pickup codes.
  • To send the stall the order it needs to prepare (your first name, items and pickup code).
  • To notify you about your order (for example when it is ready).
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, keep audit records of payments and refunds, and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The stall preparing your order: your first name, the items and the pickup code.
  • Service providers that run Brunchy on our behalf, such as our payment provider, hosting provider and notification service, only as needed to provide the service.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is active. Order, payment and refund records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal purposes. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information except records we must keep by law.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time in the app.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the app (Profile → Delete my account), or request it by email. See Delete your account.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information or to correct it by writing to the contact address below.
  • You can remove Brunchy's access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps with account access).

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our database is restricted, and payments, refunds and staff actions are logged.

Children

Brunchy is intended for college students and canteen staff. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
